Projects: ChamberUp

Project ManagerProduct OwnerLive on the App Store

I led a small team to design and ship a networking app for the Sydney CBD Chamber of Commerce. It went from first idea to the Australian App Store in eleven weeks.

What it does

Members check in at partner venues, earn points, and invite each other to meet in person. Staff see activity on a dashboard.

My role

Planning, priorities, sponsor communication, and running the build with AI-assisted tools.

Why it matters

It turns a monthly networking event into something members use all month long.

Timeline

Week 1: choose the build platform and agree the scope with the sponsor. Week 3: first working login and venue list. Week 6: check-ins, points and the member directory.

What I decided

Email-code login only, no passwords. Venue codes instead of QR codes. A private review path for the Apple App Store.

What I learned

Write the rules down before building. Test on a real phone early. Keep the sponsor close, and keep the first version small.

Links

ChamberUp on the App Store. The privacy policy. The handover guide for the Chamber.

Numbers

Eleven weeks from first meeting to launch. More than twenty members at the pilot. One live app, five partner venues.

Thanks

To the Chamber team and my teammates, who made it possible.
